Why join

Research feels clearer when the next step is visible.

The workshop programme is designed for students who are planning, conducting, or communicating an FYP. Each session focuses on a practical decision you can apply to your own project.

This case-study prototype presents proposed themes. Final session details must be checked against official UIC announcements.

Communicate clearly

Connect analysis, findings, and presentation into a coherent story.
