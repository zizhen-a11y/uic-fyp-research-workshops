Why join

Research feels clearer when the next step is visible.

The workshop programme is designed for students who are planning, conducting, or communicating an FYP. Each session focuses on a practical decision you can apply to your own project.

This case-study prototype presents proposed themes. Final session details must be checked against official UIC announcements.

01

Frame the project

Turn a broad interest into a question with a workable scope.

02

Choose evidence

Find, assess, and organise sources or data that support the study.

03

Communicate clearly

Connect analysis, findings, and presentation into a coherent story.

Common questions

Before you take part.

Official participation rules will take priority over the information shown in this prototype.

Who are the workshops for?

The proposed audience is UIC final-year undergraduate students currently working on an FYP.

Can I attend more than one session?

That depends on the final programme rules and capacity. Check the official announcement before registering.

Are the sessions online or in person?

The example schedule shows both delivery formats. The actual mode and venue remain unconfirmed.

What should I prepare?

Open a workshop's details for suggested preparation and a proposed takeaway. These activities are examples for the programme preview.

What happens when I submit this form?

The prototype saves your entry and shows a reference number. A place is only confirmed through the official UIC booking process. The prototype sends no automatic email.

Official booking, replies and email notifications

Data use, retention and deletion

Current prototype · The project database stores the form entry to demonstrate submission. Use invented names, example email addresses and sample messages. No fixed retention period or automatic deletion operates in this prototype.

Example policy · Authorised organisers would use the records to review enquiries and manage interest, keep them for up to 30 days after the programme, and delete them at the end of that period.

Example deletion request · Use a verified organiser contact, provide the entry reference, and ask for confirmation after deletion. No organiser contact or deletion request channel has been published for this prototype.
